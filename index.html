school About Gyan Inter College

Cultivating Knowledge, Character & Academic Distinction in Agra

Located in the historic and educational hub of Sikandra, Agra, Gyan Inter College was founded with an unyielding mission to provide high-quality, value-based collegiate education to aspiring students of Agra and neighboring regions.

We merge traditional disciplinary values with modern scientific temperament. Our sprawling campus accommodates spacious lecture halls, well-stocked libraries, hi-tech science and computing labs, and active student mentorship cells.
