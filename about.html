About Gyan Inter College, Sikandra, Agra

A premier institution dedicated to academic distinction, inclusive education, and character formation. Delivering undergraduate and postgraduate excellence across Arts and Sciences.

Heritage & Inception

Nurturing Generations of Enlightened Scholars in Sikandra, Agra

Gyan Inter College was established in Sikandra, Agra with a visionary purpose: to create an academic sanctuary where students from both urban and surrounding rural areas could access world-class higher education without geographical or economic limitations.

Over the years, the institution has flourished into a vibrant multidisciplinary center, renowned for high academic standards in B.A., B.Sc., M.A., and M.Sc. courses. Affiliated to the State University, the college adheres strictly to UGC criteria and the progressive pedagogical directives of the National Education Policy (NEP 2020).
